Both the creator's official pack page and the individual Poly Pizza asset page identify this asset as CC0. Verified 14 November 2026.

Changes for AFTERMARKET: removed the built-in mesh references for alternate weapon attachments so all source guns do not render together; kept their attachment transform nodes; renamed animation clips by dropping the `CharacterArmature|` prefix. The body, skeleton, animation data, and materials remain from the source. The original embedded buffer also retains unused source geometry; the asset is not represented as an original AFTERMARKET model.
